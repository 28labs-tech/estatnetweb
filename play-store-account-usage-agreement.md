## RECITALS

**A.** The Developer has developed a mobile application known as **Xyle Payments** (the "App") and wishes to publish and distribute it through the Google Play Store.

**B.** The Account Provider maintains a Google Play Console account (the "Account") and has agreed to allow the Developer to use the Account solely for the purpose of publishing and maintaining the App on the Google Play Store.

**C.** The Parties wish to set out the terms governing this arrangement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- **"App"** means the mobile application known as Xyle Payments, including all updates, versions, and related content.
- **"Account"** means the Google Play Console account owned and controlled by the Account Provider.
- **"Revenue"** means all gross income received by the Account Provider from Google LLC via the Google Play Store in connection with the App, before any taxes, fees, or deductions.
- **"Term"** means the fixed period of six (6) months as set out in Clause 2.
- **"Play Store Issue"** means any rejection, suspension, takedown, policy warning, appeal process, or any other communication or action by Google LLC affecting the App's availability on the Google Play Store.
- **"Business Day"** means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in the Republic of Uganda.

## 2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall continue for a fixed period of **six (6) months** (the "Term").

2.2 The Term is fixed and absolute. This Agreement:
   - (a) **shall not** be terminated earlier than the end of the six (6) month period by either Party for any reason; and
   - (b) **shall not** be extended or renewed beyond the six (6) month period unless a separate written agreement is executed by both Parties.

2.3 Upon expiry of the Term, the Account Provider shall cooperate in good faith to facilitate the orderly migration of the App to a Google Play Console account designated by the Developer, including but not limited to transferring ownership or providing reasonable assistance with a new listing.

## 3. LICENSE TO USE ACCOUNT

3.1 The Account Provider grants the Developer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the Account solely for the purpose of publishing, updating, and maintaining the App on the Google Play Store during the Term.

3.2 The Developer shall be given administrative access to the Account via Google Play Console's user invitation feature. The Account Provider shall not share their personal Google account login credentials with the Developer.

3.3 The Developer shall not use the Account for any application other than the App.

3.4 All rights not expressly granted herein are reserved by the Account Provider.

## 4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE ACCOUNT PROVIDER

The Account Provider covenants and agrees to:

4.1 **Serious Commitment:** Take the publication and maintenance of the App seriously and treat it with the same level of attention as a business critical matter.

4.2 **Play Store Issues — Frontline Responsibility:** Upon discovery of any Play Store Issue affecting the App, the Account Provider shall immediately take a frontline role in addressing the issue. This includes but is not limited to:
   - (a) Submitting appeals to Google LLC without delay;
   - (b) Responding to all communications from Google LLC;
   - (c) Providing any necessary documentation or verification requested by Google LLC;
   - (d) Attending any calls, meetings, or proceedings required by Google LLC.

4.3 **Response Time:** The Account Provider shall respond to any communication from the Developer regarding a Play Store Issue within **four (4) hours** during Business Days and within **twelve (12) hours** on weekends and public holidays.

4.4 **Availability:** The Account Provider shall remain available and accessible throughout the Term. The Account Provider shall not:
   - (a) Delay, ignore, or deprioritize Play Store Issues;
   - (b) Use phrases such as "let me check", "let me see", or similar equivocal language as a means of deferring action;
   - (c) Be unreachable for more than twenty-four (24) consecutive hours without prior notice to the Developer.

4.5 **No Interference:** The Account Provider shall not make any changes to the App's listing, settings, pricing, or distribution on the Google Play Store without the Developer's prior written approval.

4.6 **Account Security:** The Account Provider shall maintain the security of the Account, enable two-factor authentication, and immediately notify the Developer of any suspected security breach.

## 5. OBLIGATIONS OF THE DEVELOPER

The Developer covenants and agrees to:

5.1 Develop, maintain, and update the App in compliance with Google Play Store policies and all applicable laws.

5.2 Handle all technical development, bug fixes, and non-Play-Store issues related to the App.

5.3 Keep the Account Provider reasonably informed about the status of the App and any anticipated Play Store submissions.

5.4 Not upload any content that is illegal, infringes third-party rights, or would reasonably be expected to result in the suspension or termination of the Account.

## 6. REVENUE SHARING

6.1 All Revenue generated from the App through the Google Play Store during the Term shall be shared between the Parties as follows:
   - (a) **Ninety percent (90%)** to the Developer; and
   - (b) **Ten percent (10%)** to the Account Provider.

6.2 The Account Provider shall receive all Revenue from Google LLC and shall pay the Developer their share within **fourteen (14) Business Days** of receipt of each payment from Google LLC.

6.3 The Account Provider shall provide the Developer with a true and accurate statement showing:
   - (a) The gross Revenue received from Google LLC;
   - (b) Any deductions (e.g., taxes, Google's commission, refunds); and
   - (c) The net amount payable to the Developer.

6.4 The Developer shall have the right to audit the Account Provider's records relating to the Revenue upon reasonable notice, not more than once per quarter.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 The Developer retains all right, title, and interest in and to:
   - (a) The App and all its source code, design, graphics, content, and branding;
   - (b) The "Xyle Payments" name, logo, trademarks, and trade dress; and
   - (c) All intellectual property rights therein.

7.2 The Account Provider shall not acquire any intellectual property rights in the App by virtue of this Agreement.

7.3 Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a transfer or assignment of any intellectual property rights from either Party to the other, except as expressly provided herein.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each Party (the "Receiving Party") agrees to hold in confidence and not disclose to any third party any confidential information of the other Party (the "Disclosing Party") that is disclosed in connection with this Agreement.

8.2 Confidential information includes but is not limited to:
   - (a) The terms of this Agreement;
   - (b) The App's source code, business logic, and technical architecture;
   - (c) Revenue figures and financial data;
   - (d) Play Store account credentials and security information;
   - (e) Any business plans, strategies, or customer data.

8.3 The obligation of confidentiality shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of **three (3) years**.

8.4 This clause shall not apply to information that:
   - (a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;
   - (b) Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; or
   - (c) Is required to be disclosed by law or court order.

## 9. NON-CIRCUMVENTION

9.1 Neither Party shall, during the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, directly or indirectly:
   - (a) Circumvent the other Party to enter into any arrangement, agreement, or transaction relating to the App or the Account; or
   - (b) Solicit, induce, or encourage any third party to circumvent the other Party.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, including but not limited to loss of profits, loss of data, or loss of business opportunity.

10.2 The Account Provider shall not be liable for any Play Store Issue arising from Google LLC's policies, decisions, or actions.

10.3 The Developer's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Revenue received from the App during the Term.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 The Developer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Account Provider from and against any claims, damages, losses, or expenses arising out of:
   - (a) The Developer's use of the Account in breach of this Agreement;
   - (b) Any third-party claim that the App infringes intellectual property rights;
   - (c) The Developer's violation of applicable law or Google Play Store policies.

11.2 The Account Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Developer from and against any claims, damages, losses, or expenses arising out of:
   - (a) The Account Provider's breach of security or unauthorized access to the Account;
   - (b) The Account Provider's failure to comply with obligations under Clause 4;
   - (c) Any pre-existing issues or liabilities associated with the Account.

## 12. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance due to circumstances beyond their reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, government action, or failure of Google LLC's services.

## 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be referred to informal negotiation between the Parties for a period of **fourteen (14) days**.

13.2 If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation, the Parties shall submit the dispute to mediation at a mutually agreed mediation centre in Kampala, Uganda.

13.3 If mediation fails, either Party may refer the dispute to the courts of the Republic of Uganda.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **Republic of Uganda**.

## 15. INDEPENDENT PARTIES

## 17. AMENDMENT
